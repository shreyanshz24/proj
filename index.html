The method

How OrgFit works

Answer questions about how you prefer to work. The application calculates scores across eight dimensions, compares them with six illustrative organisational profiles, then uses a weighted-distance formula to rank the matches.

This is a reflection tool, not a psychological diagnosis, hiring assessment or prediction of career success.
